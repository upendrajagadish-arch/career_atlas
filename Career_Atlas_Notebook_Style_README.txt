CAREER ATLAS — NOTEBOOK-STYLE CLICK-TO-EXPAND MIND MAP
Updated 2026-10-11

OPEN
Double-click Career_Atlas_Notebook_Style_Click_to_Expand_Mind_Map.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. No server or installation needed. The map works offline; official roadmap and JD links require internet.

NAVIGATION
1. Starts with ONE central node. Click it to show Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai and the optional roadmap library.
2. Click a city -> company category -> alphabetical group -> company -> suggested or sourced fresher role.
3. Expand Interview rounds -> an illustrative round -> preparation syllabus or related skill roadmaps.
4. Or expand Skills & roadmaps -> desired JD skill -> roadmap stages open automatically -> topic -> details & next concept.
5. Or expand 80-hour teaching plan -> week -> weekday -> one-hour lectures. Download CSV from the plan details panel.
6. Click an expanded node again to collapse; opening another sibling collapses the previous branch. Use company search to jump directly to a company.
7. Pan by dragging the canvas, zoom with scroll or +/- buttons; Fit view reframes visible nodes. Click a leaf to see meaningful notes on the right.

DATA
466 distinct company names from the uploaded directory and JD workbook; 47 JD evidence records; 31 role and 59 skill roadmap links. The directory is not a live vacancy tracker.
Fresher role titles without sourced JD are training suggestions, not verified openings. The four displayed recruitment rounds are illustrative only, not real company round counts.
Roadmap learning phases are independently authored summaries; linked official pages hold complete published roadmaps. Not all mappings represent exact roadmap title matches. Detailed topic explanations can be reused for lectures, but are not quotations of the original roadmap diagrams.
The timetable is 20 working days x 4 lecture hours = 80 hours. No practice, assignment, lab, mock interview or assessment blocks are included in the timetable.
